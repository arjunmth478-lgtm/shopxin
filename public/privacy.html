Privacy Policy

Shopnix stores the name and email provided during account registration to support customer login. Store products, categories and settings are stored in the Shopnix database. Website visits are counted for a total visitor statistic.

When you choose WhatsApp or Telegram to place an order, your device opens the selected third-party service. Your order message is sent to that service only when you choose to send it. Please review the privacy policies of those services.

Contact the store owner through the contact details shown on the homepage for privacy questions. Replace this starter policy with terms that accurately reflect your business before public launch.
